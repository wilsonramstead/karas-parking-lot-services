What We Do

Parking Lot Work, Start to Finish

From a single driveway to a full commercial lot, Karas does the whole job — seal it, stripe it, patch it, and mark it — and finishes it clean so it looks right and lasts.

Sealcoating

Lots and driveways sealed to lock out water and UV, restore that deep black finish, and add years of life before the surface needs repaving.

Line Striping & ADA

Fresh stall lines, arrows, fire lanes, and ADA / handicap markings striped crisp, straight, and to code — new layouts or re-striping worn lots.

Commercial, HOA & Church Lots

Business, community, and church parking lots resealed and re-striped — large projects planned around your traffic and schedule, weekends included.

Pothole & Crack Repair

Potholes filled, low spots leveled, and cracks sealed before they spread — the fixes that keep a good lot from failing, done ahead of the seal and stripe.
